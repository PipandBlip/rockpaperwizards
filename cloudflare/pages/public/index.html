    How to play

    Start here

    1. Move, dash, and let the wand aim

      WASD to move, Shift to dash. Your wizard will auto target enemies, but you can press Tab to cycle targets in multiplayer modes.

    2. Cast spells, tap or charge

      Cast spells with YUIHJK. Tap it to cast a quick spell, or hold the spell to increase its power.

    3. Know your spells
      • Y Spark — fast and cheap spell.
      • U Rive — one missile, or five when full charge.
      • I Hexstone — plows through weaker spells and the Ward.
      • H Ward — create a shield against weaker spells.
      • J Beam — slow charge, but powerful beam. Can be resisted with counter beam.
      • K Grasp — hold to carry objects as a shield, or throw as a weapon.
