How to play

Start here

  1. Move, and let the wand aim

    WASD to move. Your wand points itself at whoever you are targeting — Tab switches target, Shift dashes every three seconds.

  2. Tap to cast, hold to charge

    Tap a spell key for a quick one; hold it to charge and release to let go. Charging costs more mana and buys more weight.

    Y Spark · U Rive · I Hexstone · H Ward · J Beam · K Grasp

  3. Answer with something heavier

    Every spell carries a weight. When two collide the heavier one wins and flies on, lighter by whatever it just ate. That is the whole game — read what is coming and out-weigh it.

Then it clicks

Counters pay

Stop a spell and you get mana back on the spot, then three seconds of gold corona and doubled regeneration. Get out-weighed and you get nothing.

Ward is cover, not a shield

It has a health bar set by how long you charged it, and it thins the whole time it stands. It stops Spark and Rive only — a Hexstone, a beam or a thrown crate goes straight through and takes it with them. It guards the way you face, and nothing behind you.

The beam is the ultimate

Slow to light and expensive to hold, and it eats everything it touches. When two beams meet, the orb between them slides toward whoever has less mana — let it reach you and it hurts far worse than the beam.

The fine print

The room

Stone and pillars never break. Everything wooden does — and a beam vaporises furniture in well under a second. Lattice screens stop bodies and beams but spells fly straight through the gaps, so they are a firing position, not a hiding place. Chairs and stools are not cover at all, they are ammunition.

Grasp holds furniture in front of you as a shield; release to throw it.

Modes

Three tiers of rival — Apprentice easy, Adept medium, Archmage hard — then Escalation: one at a time, then pairs working up the tiers, then threes. A wave only arrives once you have cleared the last.

Multiplayer puts up to six wizards in a free-for-all. Host a duel for a four-letter invite code; anyone who pastes it takes a seat, and bots fill whatever is left.

Odds and ends

Rive throws one missile at a tap and five at full charge. A beam costs 18 mana to ignite, 31 a second to hold, and leaves the wand scorched for a moment after you let go.

P pause · R restart. First to two rounds takes the duel.
